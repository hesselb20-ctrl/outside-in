Questions

Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and downgrades apply at the end of the billing period. Your invoices, clients and payment history stay exactly where they are when you move between plans.
Which payment methods do my clients get?
Cards, bank transfer and direct debit, processed through our payments partner. Payouts land in your bank account on a rolling two-day schedule in most countries.
Do you support more than one currency?
Every plan can invoice in over thirty currencies. Reconciliation across currencies uses the rate on the day the payment settles, and the difference is booked automatically.
Where is my data stored?
In the European Union, encrypted at rest and in transit. Exports to your accounting package run nightly, and you can download everything as a spreadsheet at any time.
